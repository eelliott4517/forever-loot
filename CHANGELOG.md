# Changelog

## 1.6.0

- **Where it drops, on every item tooltip.** Bags, chat links, the auction house and loot windows now show which bosses drop an item (with drop chances), which quest gives it, and which profession makes it. `/fl tooltip` turns it off.
- **My class** and **Hide Classic** filters on the Dungeons, Raids and Sets tabs. My class keeps the gear your class can use: your armor types, your weapon skills, shields and relics where they apply, and class-only items for your class. Hide Classic drops loot that isn't in Forever's game data. Both apply to searches too, and each character keeps its own.
- **Dungeon and raid quests**, with their rewards, in a Quests section at the bottom of each dungeon and raid. Quests for the other faction are left out. Quest rewards also show up in searches and on the wishlist.
- **Sets tab**: every item set with a piece in the dungeon, raid or quest loot, with its set bonuses and where each piece comes from. A red bar marks sets your class can wear.
- Item tooltips bundled with the addon now show set pieces, set bonuses and class restrictions on their own lines.
- Data refreshed from Wowhead and wowtbc.gg.

## 1.5.0

- Raids tab: Forever's new raids (The Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit), Onyxia's Lair, and the Classic raids with their Classic loot and drop chances.

## 1.4.0 and earlier

- Professions tab, search with Slot and Type filters, and a per-character wishlist with loot alerts.
- Classic loot in the original dungeons, new Forever drops from wowtbc.gg and Mobalytics, and bundled stats for items the beta server doesn't send.
- Dungeon loot tables and level ranges, the minimap button, and loot recorded from your own kills.
