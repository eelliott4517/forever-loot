# Changelog

## 1.7.0

- **Everything starts collapsed and opens on click.** Bosses are headers now: click one for its drops, and right-click it for its Wowhead link. The same goes for each quest on the Dungeons and Raids tabs (click for where it starts and its rewards), and for search results, the Wishlist's groups, a set's bonuses and pieces, a profession's groups and the Quests tab. Closed headers say how many items or quests they hold. Shift-click any header to open or close every section, including the bosses inside wings.
- **A Quests tab.** Every dungeon and raid quest, with where it stands for you and how to get it. Open a quest to see who starts it and where, where to turn it in, its optional lead-in, every quest that comes before it in order with your progress on each, and its rewards. The Defias Brotherhood shows its six earlier quests from Westfall to Stormwind and back; the longest, Return to Bodley in Blackrock Spire, has 24. 328 of the 626 dungeon and raid quests need earlier quests.
- **"Quests you can do"**, the tab's first page: the dungeon and raid quests you can pick up now, have in your log, or can work toward now, with the next quest to pick up under each and who gives it. Grey quests too easy for you are left out.
- **Map pins.** The pin button next to a quest giver puts the game's map pin, and its arrow in the world, on them. A TomTom arrow too, when TomTom is installed. 603 quest givers have a spot.
- Each quest shows the mark a quest giver would: a yellow "!" when you can pick it up, a grey one when it needs earlier quests, a higher level, more reputation or a profession skill, a "?" in your log (yellow when it's ready to turn in), a check when you've done it. It says what's missing: "3 quests first", "At level 30", "Honored with Argent Dawn" (the Naxxramas attunements, Zandalar Tribe quests, the Thorium Brotherhood's), "Blacksmithing 275". Lead-in quests close once you've taken what they lead to, taking one of two alternatives closes the other, and quests for another race or class are left out. The marks update as your quest log changes.
- Levels are Forever's own (A Fine Mess needs 20, not Classic's 24).
- On the Dungeons and Raids tabs, an open quest has a line saying who starts it, where, and how many quests come before it. Click it for the whole chain on the Quests tab. The quest's tooltip names the next quest to pick up, or the one in your log and who takes it in. Quests for another race or class are left out there too.
- Items that start a quest are no longer listed as quests, including the ones with a faction (Strange Water Globe, Small Scroll, Tattered Note, Head of Nefarian, An Unsent Letter, Shattered Necklace). Quests nobody can pick up in Forever are left out: Waking Naralex, When Dreams Turn to Nightmares, and old copies of Baron Aquanis, The Glowing Shard, The Last Element and Attunement to the Core. The live versions of The Glowing Shard and Attunement to the Core are listed now.
- Quests Forever added that start from an item say which: Crest of Lordaeron, Bloodied Insignia, the Abominable Head (Unending Torment) and others.
- New quests: Starving Arcane and Heart of Disruption in Dalaran, and Past Due in Scarlet Monastery.
- New drops: Golemsight Long Gun (Relic Guardian), Thermaplugg Medal of Honor (Crowd Pummeler 9-60), Quilrager Throwing Axe (Agathelos the Raging), Thorncursed Grips and Death Prophet Spine (Aggem Thorncurse), and Ebony Boneclub (Razorfen Downs trash). Six drops whose boss wasn't known are now on their bosses: Thornweaver Drape and Geomancer Headdress (Roogug), Quillord Mail Leggings (Overlord Ramtusk), Painwalker Buckler (Interrogator Vishas), Polished Skullcap (Bloodmage Thalnos) and Spellsever Crossbow (Arcanist Doan). Earthen Echo and Prehistoric Prism list their shared rewards.
- Data refreshed from Wowhead and wowtbc.gg (October 4). Quest chains come from cMaNGOS's Classic quest data and Wowhead's Forever quest pages. No site has loot for Dalaran, The Drowned City, The Barrow Deeps or Hyjal Summit's bosses yet.

## 1.6.4

- **Item tooltips use Forever's own wording.** Hit, critical strike, dodge, block, defense and attack power now read as the game shows them, such as "Equip: Improves your chance to get a critical strike by 1.0%." instead of "Increases your critical strike by 14." Set bonuses changed the same way. Wowhead's "Dropped by" and "Drop Chance" notes no longer show up in them.
- Excavation Site: Saltspine, Shadetooth and the Relic Guardian list their loot (8 items), and its 14 quests are listed with their rewards.
- City of Dalaran lists its quests (A Green Sample, Power Overwhelming, Source of Power, The Grave Knight and Opportunistic Education) and the Arcanic Enigma. The Drowned City lists Zun'Alai, Gill and Zin'aka. No loot is known for those bosses yet.
- Drops confirmed on their bosses: Demolition Girdle (Kam Deepfury), Boneslicer and Graverobber's Shovel (Dextren Ward) in The Stockade, Spiked Shell Band (Ghamoo-ra) in Blackfathom Deeps, and the Deadmines blueprints (Repair Bot from Sneed, Anvil from Gilnid, Cookie's Feast from Cookie).
- New quest rewards: Dreamer's Chestguard (Baron Aquanis), Boar Signet (Willix the Importer) and Nail Spitter (An Unholy Alliance).
- 33 Classic items Forever kept now show their Forever versions, among them Agamaggan's Clutch, Vibroblade and Forcestone Buckler. 15 items are rare now instead of uncommon. Rotmender's Robes is Rotmender's Garb, and Rotmender's Raiment has its Forever set bonuses.
- Items that start a quest are no longer listed as quests: Glowing Shard, Pendant of Myzrael, Heart of Hakkar, Eye of C'Thun, the Dire Maul class books and 8 others.
- A Season of Discovery quest that Wowhead files under Blackfathom Deeps (The Heart of the Void) is left out.
- Data refreshed from Wowhead and wowtbc.gg (October 2).

## 1.6.3

- **Crafted sets on the Sets tab.** Forever's Artisan's Tier sets (Battlegear of Glory, the Grovekeeper and Spiritcaller sets, Justice, Conviction and the rest) and the Classic crafted sets (Devilsaur Armor, Black Dragon Mail, Imperial Plate and more) are listed now: 32 more sets. A tier set shows its pieces and the crafted versions of them, with the profession that makes each.
- 68 new Forever recipes now show the item they make, with its stats, on the Professions tab and in item tooltips.
- 61 new patterns say where to get them: Vayn Moongaze on Zephras Isle, and Mishta and Vargus in Silithus.
- Excavation Site and Dalaran list their bosses, taken from the game client. No loot is known there yet.
- New drops confirmed by wowtbc.gg and Mobalytics: Cursed Murloc Eye (Gelihast) and Twilight Maul (Lorgus Jett) in Blackfathom Deeps, Death Prophet Spine (Agathelos the Raging) in Razorfen Kraul, and Nightskulker Ring (Targorr the Dread) and Repurposed Rack (Hamhock) in The Stockade.
- More quest rewards: Unending Torment (the Horde side of Abominable Creatures), A Frightened Request, The Wrath of Rath'mael and Remember That I Love You in Ruins of Lordaeron, Hidden Enemies in Ragefire Chasm, and The Test of Righteousness and The Orb of Soran'ruk in Shadowfang Keep.
- A quest chain whose steps share a name is listed once.
- Fenrus' Hide, Beetle Clasps and Prelacy Cape are no longer marked New. They're original items that Forever reworked.
- Data refreshed from Wowhead and wowtbc.gg (September 29).

## 1.6.2

- **A native look.** The window is now built from the game's own parts, so it matches WoW: Forever's interface: a portrait frame with the title and close button, the icon tabs down the right edge that the Character and Collections windows use, Blizzard's search box, dropdown menus, checkboxes and scroll bars, the recipe list's rows and section headers, item icons with quality borders, and the game's popup for Wowhead links. Text uses the game's gold, white and grey.
- Dungeon level ranges are colored by how hard the dungeon is for you, and quest names by how hard the quest is, as in the quest log. What's yours (dungeons at your level, your professions, sets your class can wear) has a gold name instead of a red bar, and wishlist items get the auction house's favorite star.
- Item levels are gone: the addon's item tooltips no longer show them, and neither does the Sets tab.

## 1.6.1

- **Your profession skills show up now.** The Professions tab's skill colors, the red bar on professions you know, "Your skill" and opening on your own profession never worked on Forever, which keeps the skills list in a different place than Classic. They do now, in any client language, and professions under a collapsed header in your skills panel count too.
- Shift-clicking an enchant now links it in chat. With the chat box closed it tells you to open it.
- Loot recorded from your own kills is credited only to the encounter's own bosses. Trash looted in the minutes after a boss fight no longer shows up as that boss's drop.
- A wishlist item several bosses drop is one row ("Lucifron +2"), counted once.
- Item tooltips, set pieces and the wishlist no longer list quest rewards from the other faction's quests.
- The Sets tab doesn't reopen a set your filters hide.
- The window now opens on the same layer as Blizzard's windows, so the dressing room (Ctrl-click) and other windows come up in front of it.

## 1.6.0

- **Where it drops, on every item tooltip.** Bags, chat links, the auction house and loot windows now show which bosses drop an item (with drop chances), which quest gives it, and which profession makes it. `/fl tooltip` turns it off.
- **My class** and **Hide Classic** filters on the Dungeons, Raids and Sets tabs. My class keeps the gear your class can use: your armor types, your weapon skills, shields and relics where they apply, and class-only items for your class. Hide Classic drops loot that isn't in Forever's game data. Both apply to searches too, and each character keeps its own.
- **Dungeon and raid quests**, with their rewards, in a Quests section at the bottom of each dungeon and raid. Quests for the other faction are left out. Quest rewards also show up in searches and on the wishlist.
- **Sets tab**: every item set with a piece in the dungeon, raid or quest loot, with its set bonuses and where each piece comes from. A red bar marks sets your class can wear.
- Item tooltips bundled with the addon now show set pieces, set bonuses and class restrictions on their own lines.
- Data refreshed from Wowhead and wowtbc.gg.

## 1.5.0

- Raids tab: Forever's new raids (The Barrow Deeps, Hyjal Summit), Onyxia's Lair, and the Classic raids with their Classic loot and drop chances.
